About Government Scheme Navigator AI

Empowering citizens and students to easily locate, learn about, and apply for government welfare initiatives.

Our Mission

Many eligible citizens and students miss out on government schemes, subsidies, and scholarships due to complex portals, search friction, and language barriers. Our mission is to bridge this information gap by offering an intelligent, multi-lingual, and conversation-driven Navigator that resolves citizen profile requirements instantly.

Technology Stack

The Government Scheme Navigator AI is constructed using state-of-the-art serverless technologies:

  • Frontend: Responsive HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 with Glassmorphism styles, and ES6 JavaScript.
  • Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL) for scheme catalog caching, profiles, and chat log storage.
  • AI Engine: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash accessed via secure serverless proxy to parse intents and generate natural language recommendations.
  • Hosting: Netlify deployment with Serverless API functions to safeguard API credentials.

Key Features

  • Natural Language Chat: Engage with the chatbot as if talking to a helper.
  • Bilingual Capability: Automatically detects English and Tamil queries, responding back in the corresponding language.
  • Smart Onboarding Profile: Tailors recommendations automatically based on Name, Age, State, and Occupation.
  • Official Links & Sources: Displays verifying sources and links directly for authenticity.
  • Admin Dashboard: Direct CRUD capabilities to add, edit, or delete schemes instantly from the portal.

Future Map

We plan to integrate Supabase pgvector in the next release to enable AI-powered semantic similarity search, expanding scheme discovery to match conceptual matches rather than strict keyword combinations. Additionally, we aim to implement push notifications for schemes matching user profiles as they get added.
